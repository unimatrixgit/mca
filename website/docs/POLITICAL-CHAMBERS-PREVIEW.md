# Political chambers — website design preview
Owner scope: under User Panel / Politics & Gameplay, Landsraad contains House Elections and Imperial Elections. Imperial Command is the Emperor's water/spice rights workspace, with separate Water and Spice pages. All use shared Dev design and existing Minecraft-themed artwork.

This iteration is website scaffolding. No votes, nominations, office changes, rights grants or game writes. Submission controls remain disabled. Owner explicitly requested fictional demo data for design refinement: candidate names, descending example vote totals and House support are visibly labelled DEMO. Local radio selection, motivation expansion, pagination and Imperial allocation controls are interactive but never submitted or saved.

House context is resolved from the authenticated account's verified Minecraft UUID, through the existing private gateway's read-only faction endpoint. Only the verified own faction appears. Bank fields are discarded and never returned. Missing/unavailable faction produces a closed chamber, never an all-Houses fallback. The existing read-only office projection identifies the Emperor. Website ranks do not confer that political office.

Planned flow: nomination, ballot, result; imperial rights: recipient, scope, term, decree, confirmation, audited register. These are design placeholders, not approved rules. Before activation define candidature/voter eligibility, election schedules, ties, faction changes, term transitions, and rights/revocation semantics. Revalidate faction/office server-side for every future write.

Acceptance: six navigable pages with shared artwork/tabs; own-House-only projection; no identity overrides or write methods; unavailable and unlink states; responsive desktop/mobile layout; no game changes. Rollout requires reviewed fork merge, backups and live page checks.

Owner addition: The Landsraad overview includes a Force elections card, divided into Start a rally (reason and action) and House agreement. It is not displayed on House Elections. Two distinct Houses must agree before a new election is held; repeated endorsements from one House do not increase the count. This remains a disabled design preview, with no invented rally or support totals. Before activation, establish who can represent a House, whether the initiating House counts toward the two, and the target/expiry rules for a rally.

Owner addition: Imperial Command has a Counter rally card. Support from two distinct Houses denies the proposed election; repeated endorsements by one House count once. Landsraad also has Start revolt, with thresholds and consequences deliberately undefined. Both remain disabled website previews. Before activation define timing/precedence when election and counter-rally thresholds are both reached, House representation, and revolt rules.

Election preview: application motivation, acceptance checkbox and disabled submit; 12 fictional candidates sorted by descending demo vote totals, nine per page, with CSS Minecraft-style pixel heads and expandable motivations. House members select one leader candidate from their own faction chamber. Everyone may apply for Emperor. House leaders have five Imperial votes distributed across candidates or all on one candidate. The demo allocation is accessible for review, not evidence of leadership or voting authority. Votes for other Imperial voters remain unspecified. No real identities are fabricated as candidates. Demo selection persists across candidate pages, but resets on page reload/navigation.

Owner war rule: Landsraad has Declare war on House, requiring Imperial support before a declaration proceeds. The Veiled Concord (replacement name for the requested Bene Gesserit-style faction) cannot declare war, but may suggest another House declare war against a target. A recommendation is not a declaration or an approval. Displayed Amber/Jade Houses, dispute and pending support are fictional demo data. Both paths remain disabled; there are no war state writes or notifications. Before activation, define who represents a House and enforce the Concord restriction and Emperor approval server-side.

Latest election cadence: House terms last two weeks, with elections on Sundays at 09:00 GMT. Emperor terms last four weeks, with elections on the corresponding Sunday at 15:00 GMT. Each election page has a calendar card with four clearly labelled illustrative dates, anchored to 15 November 2026 solely for the demo. No first live date has been approved, no scheduler is activated, and GMT is fixed independently of daylight saving.

Latest pending war design rules: House declarations once per three elections (two-election cooldown); Concord suggestions once per ten elections (nine-election cooldown), faction-wide. When a House accepts the Concord suggestion, the requested reward is a free Sardaukar-themed Legendary+ battle kit including armour and weapons, website placeholder only. Acceptance does not waive Imperial approval. Before activation define which election type advances these counters and the exact recipient/contents of the House kit.
