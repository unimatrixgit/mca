DuneRealms is looking for Alpha testers. If you enjoy exploring new systems, trying unusual combinations and helping a community take shape, we would love to have you aboard.

This is an early Alpha, not a finished release. Some features are playable, others are still being developed, and rough edges are part of the journey. You do not need to buy anything to volunteer.

## What we are building

The Quest for Tavren II Aedrecht brings together a desert science-fiction Minecraft network and a connected community website. Our direction includes:

- A lobby and connected worlds, with travel between them through the network.
- Java and Bedrock access, linked website accounts and a shared player identity.
- Custom menus, jobs, spice trading, inventory systems and a Dunemark economy.
- Clans, clan banking, kit design and player trading.
- Quests, discoveries and longer-term progression, with more world and political systems planned.
- A connected website, forums and Support Center that make it easier to follow your journey and get help.

This describes the direction of the project; it is not a claim that every system is complete. The next stages focus on more polished menus, stronger Java/Bedrock compatibility, clearer kit and clan tools, and balanced quest progression.

## What we need help testing

- First-time setup: registration, linking a Minecraft account, joining and reaching the lobby.
- Java and Bedrock differences: controls, menus, text, images and actions that behave differently between clients.
- Travel: choosing destinations, arriving safely and returning to the lobby.
- Inventory and economy: items, balances, trading and what happens when you reconnect or change worlds.
- Menus and everyday tasks: unclear labels, dead buttons, missing options and too many steps.
- Clan and kit features as they become available: permissions, previews, ownership and ordinary player workflows.
- Website and Support: mobile layouts, account navigation and reporting a problem.

New players are especially useful: the things an experienced administrator overlooks are often the first things a newcomer notices. You do not need previous testing experience, and occasional testers are welcome.

## How to sign up

[Sign up for Alpha testing](https://support.dunerealms.com/alpha-signup.php)

The signup opens a private Support ticket. Tell us your Minecraft username, whether you play Java or Bedrock, your platform, a rough idea of your available time and what you would like to test. In the ticket details, add a few words about why you are interested. Our Support team will review your application and reply with the next steps.

Use your DuneRealms account to sign in. You can create or manage it in the [Account Center](https://cp.dunerealms.com/).

## What makes a useful bug report?

Tell us which edition and world you were using, the steps that led to the problem, what you expected and what actually happened. A screenshot or short video helps when the problem is visual. Mention whether you can reproduce it after reconnecting. Please leave passwords, payment details and unrelated personal information out of reports.
