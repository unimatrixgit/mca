# Builders wanted — help shape the worlds of DuneRealms

Every great realm begins with someone placing the first block. DuneRealms is looking for creative builders to help turn our worlds into places worth exploring.

From the streets of Arrakeen to hidden sietches, imposing palaces and busy starports, there is plenty left to imagine. We want builders who enjoy atmosphere, thoughtful detail and making a place feel alive.

## What could you help build?

- Desert cities, villages and settlements.
- Sietches, underground chambers and believable interiors.
- Palaces, landmarks and story locations.
- Starports, public spaces and the routes connecting them.
- Landscaping and the small details that bring a world together.

You do not need to know every page of the lore. Show us your style, your ideas and what you enjoy building. A few screenshots or links to your own work are welcome, and rough availability is enough: you do not need to promise a fixed schedule.

## Join the Builder Team

Accepted builders receive the Builder rank and its Builder Team seal. Contribution is recognised separately: staff can award the Builder Award for work that helps shape the realm. Applying, or holding the rank, does not automatically earn that award.

Our Builder application uses the same familiar application flow as Alpha testing, with questions tailored to building. Applications are private and reviewed by staff.
