# Your guide to the DuneRealms Support Center

Every journey encounters a blocked passage. The DuneRealms Support Center gives you one place to ask for help, request a review and follow the response from the right team.

Open the [Support Center](https://support.dunerealms.com/) and choose the card that matches your request. The page now puts parental and privacy requests first, followed by general support, ban appeals and Alpha tester applications.

## Parents and guardians

Use Parental requests for consent questions or help concerning a child's account. A separate link handles requests to remove a child's account data. These requests go to the Legal department for manual review. Submitting a form does not automatically establish guardianship, grant consent or change access.

Provide the relevant account or Minecraft username and explain what you need. Do not attach passwords, government identity documents or unrelated personal information. Staff will explain any further verification through the private ticket.

## Privacy and account-data removal

The GDPR data removal card opens a dedicated Legal request. Identify the account concerned and describe the data or account action you are requesting. The team checks ownership and the scope of the request before taking action. This is a request for review, not an automatic deletion button or a promise that every historical record will be erased.

## General support

For account, website or game problems, choose General support. Include what you expected, what happened instead, where and when it happened, and what you already tried. Exact error messages and relevant screenshots make reports easier to reproduce. Some gameplay requests require a verified Minecraft link; the form will explain the applicable requirements.

## Ban appeals

Open Appeal a ban and enter the ban ID. The form links your appeal to the original moderation record so staff can review the correct case. Explain the situation and provide relevant evidence. An appeal is private; evidence and ticket conversations are not published on the public ban list.

Submitting an appeal does not remove an active ban. The public register shows PENDING while an appeal awaits a decision, then APPROVED or DENIED when that decision is recorded. The ban's Active or Unbanned status is shown separately. Other bans on the same player can still apply. Closing a ticket alone is not an approval.

## Help test the Alpha

Want to help improve the realms? The Alpha tester card accepts applications from Java and Bedrock players. Tell us what you would like to test and how you can help. Support reviews applications manually; applying does not automatically grant a role or reward.

## Follow your request

Use your DuneRealms account and the ticket-status link to return to existing requests. Keep follow-up information in the same ticket so the team has the full context. Requests are available to the requester and authorised staff; public moderation cards show only the public ban information and appeal status.
