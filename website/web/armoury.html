Faction passives use the recorded faction. Full-set effects additionally check that all four equipped pieces are tagged reclaimer suits belonging to your recorded faction. A mixed set can protect against sun exposure if every piece is tagged as a reclaimer suit, but does not grant a faction set bonus.

Arrival and respawning

Faction selection requests travel to the corresponding homeworld without a normal paid fare. On a proxy network, this is a server-transfer request. On a single backend, the homeworld must already be loaded for a local arrival. Respawn is redirected to the homeworld only when that world is loaded on the current backend; there is no general cross-server respawn router.

Choosing carefully

Vrijduin suits favor harvesting and sneaking; Harkveen and Korenholt favor combat; Aerdrecht favor speed and extra full-set health; Oordwijk favor short concealment and explosives. Vaerkring has appearance-related support but is not one of the five player-selectable factions.

Reclaimer suits, flight and Oordwijk equipment

Armor that looks like leather but protects like netherite

Faction kits use colored leather reclaimer suits with custom armor attributes. Helmet, chest, legs and boots provide 3, 8, 6 and 3 armor points respectively. Each piece adds three armor toughness and 0.1 knockback resistance. Faction trim patterns distinguish the suits; ordinary armor does not acquire these properties by matching its color.

/dune kit requires a faction and adds another shovel and four armor pieces. The shovel is a tagged netherite shovel with Efficiency V and Unbreaking III. Kits do not include a general food allowance or a conventional melee weapon; rockhaven caches provide food, while weapons must be obtained separately.

Winged reclaimer suit

To use /dune elytra, wear your faction's leather reclaimer suit chestplate, carry an Elytra in storage inventory and have 1,000,000 Dunemarks. The command consumes the carried Elytra, replaces the worn chestplate with a tagged winged Elytra and charges the price. The new item retains custom chest armor protection and faction identity, and qualifies as the reclaimer suit chest slot for heat protection and set bonuses. It is a purpose-built upgrade, not a general enchantment transfer service.

Oordwijk Shadow Step and charge

With a matching full Oordwijk suit, using an Ender Pearl in the main hand grants five seconds of invisibility. The plugin temporarily stores and removes armor, then restores it, returning displaced pieces to inventory or dropping overflow. During removal you do not count as wearing a complete suit, including for sun protection.

The tagged Oordwijk Implosion Charge launches a wind charge and supplies a replacement one tick later. Its impact produces immediate primed TNT attributed to the shooter. Despite its name, it uses ordinary TNT explosion behavior; treat it as destructive explosives rather than a harmless movement tool.

Sun exposure and shelter

Arrakduin has a dedicated heatstroke timer. Default lethal exposure is 300 seconds: five minutes of uninterrupted qualifying sunlight. The plugin checks once each second and warns on the first exposed second and at thirty-second intervals.

When the timer runs

All of these conditions must apply: you are alive on Arrakduin, outside Creative and Spectator modes, not wearing a full tagged reclaimer suit, in clear weather, during daytime before world time 12300, and exposed at the top of the surface rather than beneath shelter.

The plugin shows a burning effect while exposure accumulates. Fire-tick damage caused by that active exposure effect is cancelled until the timer expires. At the lethal threshold, the plugin sets health to zero. Ordinary fire, lava and other hazards are separate; a reclaimer suit is not general fire immunity.

What resets exposure

Taking cover, wearing a full tagged suit, reaching night or otherwise ceasing to meet the exposure conditions resets the timer. You do not need to wait for gradual recovery. The stored exposure entry is removed and the associated fire effect cleared.

Arrakduin defaults to persistent daytime

DuneTerrain disables the daylight cycle on Arrakduin and sets world time to 6000 during setup. Under that default, waiting for a natural sunset is not a reliable survival plan. Use a suit or reach shelter. An operator can alter world time or game rules separately.

Equipment edge cases

A winged reclaimer suit counts as the chest piece. A mixed-faction set can still satisfy heat protection if every equipped slot has the reclaimer suit tag, but faction set bonuses require matching tags. Oordwijk Shadow Step temporarily removes armor, so its five-second concealment also temporarily breaks the full reclaimer suit condition.
