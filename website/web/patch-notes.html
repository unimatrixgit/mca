How this register works

Normal updates group 5–10 genuine completed product tasks, retaining their DUNE ticket IDs. Urgent bugfixes are published separately with the problem, repair and verification; they do not wait for a feature bundle. A deployment is not automatically a new patchnote.

This register starts with the new system. Earlier development remains in the repository and contribution record; no historical releases have been invented. Live game news remains separate.
