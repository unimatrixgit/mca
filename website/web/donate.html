DUNEREALMS.COM / COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Keep the caravans moving

Voluntary contributions help with hosting, backups and maintenance. When a community goal is reached, its fixed in-game currency reward will be available to every player, whether they contributed or not.

Donations are not open yet

We are setting up the payment provider, funding targets and public accounting. No payments are being collected through this page. We will publish the target, period, use of funds and reward before opening a campaign.

Contributing does not buy extra currency, power, access, priority or an exclusive reward. A successful monthly goal releases the same fixed currency reward to every player. This is support for an independently operated game server, not a claim of charitable status or tax deductibility.

Monthly community goals

Loading monthly goals…

Each calendar month in Europe/Brussels has its own target. Only verified contributions count. If that target is not reached during the month, its reward does not unlock. A reached goal stays unlocked; its codes have no expiry.

Public reward codes

After a goal is reached and verified, its public code appears above. No payment, receipt, donor account or mailing-list subscription will be required.

  1. Copy a public code above or an earned personal code from your account.
  2. Join the gameplay server and enter /communitycode CODE.
  3. The same fixed amount of in-game Dunemarks is credited once per Minecraft UUID for that monthly goal. A repeat claim cannot credit it again.

Codes are intended to have no expiry and no shared redemption limit, including for players who join later. Every player receives the same reward; it cannot be cashed out or redeemed for real-world value. Accounts and unlocks are local to this gameplay server.

Your website account

Sign in or register in the account control panel and verify your Minecraft UUID in game. Only goals reached after your UUID first links generate personal codes; there is no backfill. Used codes move to claim history after the game confirms the credit. Unlinking, changing names or changing website accounts does not reset claims. Public codes remain available equally to players without a linked website account.

Timing and transparency

A campaign may have a clearly stated fundraising deadline. That is separate from code redemption: published reward codes will remain claimable while the reward service operates. If a campaign ends below its target, funds still support the published running costs; no reward is promised until its goal is reached. We will publish campaign totals and explain any changed plans.
