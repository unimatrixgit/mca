# Imperial staff and department seals

Created with the built-in image_gen tool, one call per asset, on 2026-10-28. Original generated PNG files are retained under website/artwork/seals/. Production transparent PNG files at 600px, 300px, and 150px are under website/web/assets/seals/.

The master uses exact DUNEREALMS.COM, DUNEREALMS, and UNIMATRIX lettering, with an original decorative authorization flourish. Personal seals cover only Owners dunerealms, unimatrix999, grmacken, richwjr and Corestaff racoonvibz. Department seals cover Support, Ban Appeals, Forum Support, Legal, and Sales.

Use the 300px PNG at a displayed width of 150px for sharper email and forum footers. Use the manifest.json mapping in website/web/assets/seals/ for exact paths. Provide matching plain text or alt text alongside each small seal so staff roles and identifiers remain accessible.

All 11 generated PNGs passed alpha-channel validation, and the contact sheet was visually reviewed for consistency and exact labels. At small display sizes the primary names/departments are readable; the master UNIMATRIX flourish and secondary role lettering are ornamental small text.

Exact prompts and original generation paths are recorded in generation-provenance.json. Reference artwork was inspected before generation: website/web/assets/imperial-seal.png and website/artwork/branding/dunerealms-season-approved.png. Imagery itself was generated; prepare_seals.py performs only PNG optimization, resizing, alpha validation, manifest writing, and contact-sheet assembly.

No deployment or other project files were modified for this asset task.
