Applicable jurisdictions, licences, commercial activity and rights-holder enforcement intentions have not been established.

## Changes assessed

- Removed the six-essay journal, index, data, graphics metadata, browser code, styles and generator, together with navigation and public contribution-record references. The build no longer regenerates these routes.
- The three requested novel characters appeared only in that removed journal. They were not globally relabelled inside a retained retelling. A separate original supporting outline now features Tavren Sile, Mira Vesk and Archivist Elian Voss investigating conflicting cargo records.
- Public website labels now use the original proposals recorded in `PUBLIC-NAMES.json`. This covers static pages, the searchable manual, accessible image labels, SVG text, faction panels, market labels, world-status names and dynamic feed presentation.
- The visible site brand is `dunerealms.com`; spice is retained at the owner's explicit request.
- Added an unofficial-project notice and the operator contact `unimatrix@dunerealms.com` to every generated public page and the custom-host login page.
- Added the requested comparison wiki. Legacy terminology remains intentionally in its clearly labelled comparison column, also included as the first manual chapter, not as current public faction or planet labels.
- Replaced direct legacy manual PDF download links with the current web manual. Historical PDFs and prior plugin releases still exist in the repository and external distribution history.

Overall: these edits reduce direct copying and naming overlap in the website, but do **not** justify describing the project as legally cleared or low risk. The most substantial unresolved areas are the retained brand/overall presentation, asset provenance, and the unchanged game/distribution materials. No conclusion about a particular live trademark registration was reached.

## Legal sources

- [U.S. Copyright Office Circular 33](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf): individual names and short phrases are generally not copyrightable; trademark protection is a separate issue.
- [U.S. Copyright Office Circular 14](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ14.pdf): original additions do not acquire rights in underlying protected material; unauthorised adaptations may infringe.
- [USPTO: likelihood of confusion](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion): similarity and related goods/services matter to source confusion.
- [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines): require a prominent independence notice and identifiable responsibility/contact; they do not license someone else's fictional universe.
- [Official Dune overview](https://dunenovels.com/dune/): source context for the wiki's desert-setting and spice comparison.

These are background sources, primarily U.S. copyright/trademark summaries, not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.

Publication follow-up: the operator contact is now revealed only after self-hosted ALTCHA verification. Public HTML and static browser code do not contain the plain address. This protects against basic harvesting, not all automated solving or spam. Contact access controls do not affect the IP assessment above.

## 2026-10-27 commemorative note series update

At the operator's request the 1, 5, 1,000 and 5,000 Dunemark designs now explicitly portray Stilgar, Aliah, Ghanima and Leto as Minecraft-style fan characters. The 5,000 reverse imagines a victory over House Harkonnen. The 100 retains original character Mira Vesk. All notes use Vaerkring Reserve and a newly designed crown/sun/dunes imperial seal.

Assessment: the character editions reintroduce Dune adaptation references and therefore undermine any claim that all published artwork is independent of the source works. Their Minecraft styling and unofficial label do not supply adaptation rights. They are decorative game artwork, no cash value, not licensed merchandise. No percentage probability of a claim can be supported from the available evidence. This is not a jurisdiction-specific legal clearance.

The U.S. Copyright Office describes adaptations and the rights-holder's derivative-work right: https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ14.pdf (checked 2026-10-27). Trademark confusion is a separate inquiry: https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion . The new seal and invented reserve label have not undergone a trademark clearance search. A lower-risk merchandise edition would use wholly original characters and stories or obtain appropriate licences.
